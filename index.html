Quick Intro to Double-Entry Accounting

Double-entry accounting means every action is written down in two places so the books always stay balanced. One side is a Debit (Dr), the other is a Credit (Cr). They always match.

Example a 4th–6th grader gets:
You lend your friend $5.

Your books:
Dr Friend owes me    $5
Cr Cash               $5

Friend’s books:
Dr Cash               $5
Cr I owe you         $5

Every action is written on both sides so the total always balances. Nothing appears or disappears without a matching entry on the other account.

In this app we use the same idea for harms, sins, and claims. Every hit or choice creates matching entries on the ledgers.
